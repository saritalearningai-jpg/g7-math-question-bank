## Unit 4: Proportional Relationships and Percentages (7.RP.A.1, 7.RP.A.3)
### Q4.1
**Type:** Multiple Choice | **Standard:** 7.RP.A.1
A faucet leaks $\frac{5}{9}$ liter of water in $\frac{2}{5}$ hour. At this rate, what is the amount of water in liters leaked in $1$ hour?
- A) $\frac{10}{45}$
- B) $\frac{18}{25}$
- C) $\frac{43}{45}$
- D) $1 \frac{7}{18}$
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
**Correct Answer:** D) $1 \frac{7}{18}$ | **Solution:** $\frac{5}{9} \times \frac{5}{2} = \frac{25}{18} = 1 \frac{7}{18}$
</details>

## Unit 5: Rational Number Arithmetic (7.NS.A.3)
### Q5.7
**Type:** Constructed Response | **Standard:** 7.NS.A.3
Recipe: $1 \text{ cup}$ oil, $\frac{5}{8} \text{ cup}$ vinegar, $\frac{1}{2} \text{ cup}$ honey, $1 \text{ tbsp}$ mustard. ($1 \text{ cup} = 8 \text{ oz}$, $1 \text{ oz} = 2 \text{ tbsp}$).
Part A: Total fluid ounces?  
Part B: Servings if each serving is $3 \text{ tbsp}$?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
**Correct Answer:** A: $17.5 \text{ oz}$ | B: $11$ servings
**Solution:** $8 + 5 + 4 + 0.5 = 17.5 \text{ oz}$. $17.5 \times 2 = 35 \text{ tbsp}$. $35 \div 3 = 11.66... \rightarrow 11$.
</details>

## Unit 7: Angles, Triangles, and Prisms (7.G.B.6)
### Q7.4
**Type:** Multiple Choice | **Standard:** 7.G.B.6
Composite figure: Parallelogram (base $26 \text{ cm}$, height $16 \text{ cm}$) on top of a Trapezoid (bases $26 \text{ cm}$ and $40 \text{ cm}$, height $16 \text{ cm}$). Area?
- F) $1,056 \text{ cm}^2$
- G) $1,360 \text{ cm}^2$
- H) $944 \text{ cm}^2$
- J) $528 \text{ cm}^2$
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
**Correct Answer:** H) $944 \text{ cm}^2$
**Solution:** $(26 \times 16) + \frac{1}{2}(26+40)16 = 416 + 528 = 944$.
</details>

## Unit 8: Probability (7.SP.C.8)
### Q8.1
**Type:** Constructed Response | **Standard:** 7.SP.C.8
Spinner X (Red, Blue) and Spinner Y ($1, 2, 3$). What is $P(\text{Red OR Odd})$?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>
**Correct Answer:** $5/6$
**Solution:** $P(R) + P(O) - P(R \cap O) = 1/2 + 2/3 - 1/3 = 5/6$.
</details>
